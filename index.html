Air Freight Export

India's Trusted International Shipping Partner

Sayona Shipping Service is a leading international freight forwarding and logistics company headquartered in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, India. With over 30 years of combined industry expertise, we provide end-to-end shipping solutions for businesses across India looking to export and import goods worldwide.

Our comprehensive logistics services include ocean freight (FCL and LCL), air freight, customs clearance, warehousing and storage, and complete supply chain management. Whether you are shipping textiles from Tirupur, electronics from Bangalore, pharmaceuticals from Hyderabad, or automotive parts from Chennai, Sayona Shipping Service handles every step of the process.

We serve a diverse range of industries including textile and apparel, high-tech electronics, pharmaceuticals, automotive, agri products, and general cargo. Our specialized industry knowledge ensures your cargo is handled with the right care, documentation, and compliance requirements.

Sayona Shipping Service operates across 25+ countries with established trade routes connecting India to the USA, United Kingdom, Germany, UAE, China, Japan, and many more destinations. Our 98% on-time delivery rate and 500+ satisfied clients speak to our commitment to reliability and excellence.
